Our Story

Zeynel İnegöl Köftecisi has been continuing the tradition of İnegöl köfte in Bursa since 1961. This culinary journey is now carried forward by the 3rd generation.

The secret of İnegöl köfte lies in the careful preparation of quality beef and traditional cooking techniques. Each köfte prepared with years of experience and passion reflects our family's dedication to this craft.

"İşin sırrını soruyorlar, 'bir köfteye adanmış yaklaşık 50 yıl' diyoruz."

[Translation: "They ask about the secret – we say, 'about 50 years devoted to one köfte.'"] — Zeynel Organ, 2006

Today we are proud to serve at two important locations in Bursa: Kayhan Çarşı and Mobilium AVM. At each location, we continue to serve you with the same quality, same flavor, and same hospitality.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is İnegöl köftesi?

İnegöl köftesi is a regional Turkish meatball specialty from İnegöl district in Bursa, prepared with quality beef and special spices, cooked using traditional techniques with years of experience and craftsmanship.

Where are Zeynel İnegöl Köftecisi locations?

We have two locations: Kayhan Çarşı (Kayhan Cd. No:15, Osmangazi/Bursa) and Mobilium AVM (İnegöl Bursa Highway 6.km, Yeniceköy/İnegöl).

What are the opening hours?

Kayhan Çarşı is open until 22:00. Mobilium AVM is open daily from 09:00 to 23:00.
